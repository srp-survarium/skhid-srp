German personal defence weapon, ammo: 4.6x30, here in the MP7A1 version with a sound suppressor. Developed by Heckler & Koch together with its own armour-piercing cartridge and used by the Bundeswehr and police special units. Equipped with a 20-round magazine. Supports single and automatic fire. Premium version in a special camouflage finish.
