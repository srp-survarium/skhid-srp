A to Z

Terms

  • Anomaly: a zone that hurts while you stand in it. Anomalies
  • Armour: cuts a hit by how far it exceeds the penetration. Damage
  • Armour penetration: what a bullet takes off the armour first. Damage
  • Artefact: grows in anomalies; carried for its effect. Artefacts
  • Battery: carried to your container in Protective device. Batteries
  • Booster: a pocket item with a fixed bonus. Supplies
  • Bundle: items sold together for gold. Shop
  • Charger: gives batteries during a chargers match. Batteries
  • Condition: how worn an item is; repairs bring it back. Upgrades
  • Container: where a team puts its batteries. Batteries
  • Defect: a negative modification from wear or a drop. Modifications
  • Donor: the second copy an upgrade uses up. Upgrades
  • Drop: an item a match gives. Drops
  • Faction: a seller with reputation ranks. Factions
  • Gold: premium money; 1 gold = 100 silver. Silver and gold
  • Injury: an empty leg or hand, or a hit head. Healing
  • Isolation: armour against anomalies: fire, electric, toxins, radiation. Anomalies
  • Item level: how strong an item is; rooms can cap it. Rooms
  • Kit: one of your 3 loadouts. Kits
  • Late join: coming into a match already played. Rooms
  • Modification: a bonus on a weapon or gear. Modifications
  • Pain: a pool every hit drains; empty, you die. Damage
  • Premium: more silver, experience, drops. Shop
  • Rank: your reputation level with a faction. Factions
  • Room: a group that plays match after match. Rooms
  • Silver: the money of matches and the shop. Silver and gold
  • Spot: marking an enemy or a battery. Spotting
  • Tickets: a team's lives in Team Deathmatch. Game modes
  • Toxins: the pool toxic and radiation damage drain; it does not recover by itself. Healing
  • Upgrade: one more modification from a donor, or one less. Upgrades
