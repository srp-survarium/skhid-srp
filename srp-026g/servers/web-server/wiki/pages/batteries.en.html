Batteries

The Protective device modes. Where everything is: the Maps tab.

  • Each team has a container. A battery in your container is a point for your team.
  • 10 batteries a match. A team with all of them in its container wins, 3 s later.
  • Time up (15 min): more batteries wins; equal is a draw.
  • Taking a battery out of a container takes that point away.
  • Picking one up takes 0.6 s; putting it into a container 0.6 s.
  • Carrying one: movement speed -5 %.
  • Dropped or carried by a player who dies: it lies where it fell.
  • After the last battery goes in, no container can be used.

Spotting a battery

  • Aim at the battery, its body too, and press spot: your team sees it. No cooldown.
  • No marker at spawn; picking it up wipes the markers; after a drop the dropper's team sees it; a battery in a container has none.
  • Only the battery's first spotter gets the spot award. See Spotting.

Protective device

The batteries lie at their spawn points from the start.

Protective device: chargers

  • Batteries come out of chargers during the match: 2 / 3 chargers work at once.
  • A working charger gives a battery every 60 s, give or take 10 s.
  • The goal is the same: all 10 in your container.
