# AK-12 nerf of 0.27a

The owner's rules and what they change. Data: `gameplay/items/weapons/wpn_ak_12.options`
and `wpn_ak_12_premium.options`; the change ships as a resources.db patch
(`scripts/content-edit/ak12-027-1.json`).

- **Decision (the owner, 2026-10-20):** "0.27a (released 3 months after 0.26a where
  ak12 was introduced) nerfed ak12 damage 55 » 50 and armour penetration 45 » 40.
  I think it should be done here".
- **0.27's changes only (the owner, 2026-10-20):** what 0.27 changed, nothing a later
  version changed (0.28a2 cuts piercing to 0.30, 0.28d0 damage to 0.45, 0.31a2 and
  0.46c2 rescale).
- **Values:** both rifles `bullet_damage` 0.55 -> 0.5, `bullet_pierce` 0.45 -> 0.4;
  the card's damage 55 -> 50 and armour piercing 45 -> 40. Unchanged: rate of fire
  650, magazine 30, damage distances 20 / 50 (0.27d0 has 30 / 60: not in the
  owner's note, not taken).
- **The plain AK-12's damage:** 0.27d0 (the only 0.27 build at hand; no 0.27a) has
  the plain AK-12 at 0.55 and the premium at 0.5. Both are at 0.5 here; the plain
  back at 0.55 is two lines of the edit list.
- **Premium gives silver only (the owner, 2026-10-20):** the premium AK-12 is the
  plain one plus +10 % silver (default modification 1230). Its 2044 (reload time
  -15 %) and 2094 (reliability +25 %) defaults go. Kept as the item's own: name,
  description, icon, model (camo), `base_rarity_level` 3, `drop_weight` 0 (never
  dropped). The other premium weapons are not changed here.
- **Who computes it:** damage, piercing and reload time in the client's lockstep
  simulation (a `next` row of `crates/session/src/v026g/simulation_changes.tsv`);
  srp reads the defaults for wear, bonuses and rolls (`item_params`).
- **Release:** the resources.db patch, made by `scripts/content-edit/content_edit.py`
  from the edit list (with `gameplay/items/weapons/wpn_ak_12*` from the live server
  game data) and `release/build-patch`; the server game data built again with that
  patch; the `next` row. The damage calculator and the wiki rebuild per release.
