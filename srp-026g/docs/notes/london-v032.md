# London (v0.32)

The live London rebuilt from 0.32a4's own projects (the owner, 2026-11-20: "I think we want london 0.32
in the game and london 0.42 in the game"; `london.md`, "Name and build"). Content pack `london-v032@1`,
made by resource-porter's `build-survarium-near-map` in the same one pass as London (v0.42)
(`ports/survarium-032a4-london-to-026g.json`, `docs/near-builds.md` there), and the forward effects as
0.44a5 and 0.32a4 draw them with client patch 169 (pack `forward-effects@1`).

## In place, by one switch

London's maps stay what they are: map ids (181-183 day, 276-278 rain, 279-281 evening, Research, Blackout
and Free for All), level 9, its names and loading pictures. The pack adds 0.32a4's projects as files of
their own, `projects/v032a_level_09/<weather>_<mode>` (day 2-5, rain and evening 2-4, from 0.32a4's
`day_N`, `rain_N`, `evn_N`), and `london-v032-1.json` points each of London's twelve configs at them
(`mode.match_making_params.map_name`: the ten modes, Blackout's and Free for All's). Nothing else of a
config changes: its rules, music, Research units and build (`v032`) stay. The old projects
`level_09/*` and the lists that edit them are no config's any more; they go in a cleanup of their own.

## The owner's edits, on the new projects

Generated by `scripts/london-v032/make_edits.py` from what players have of London (none moved to fit the
new collision):

- `london-v032-spawns-1.json` (after `london-1.json`): each project's 20 team spawns where the owner
  placed them in the map editor (`london-1.json`; the rain and evening as their day), by full_name
  (0.32a4 names them as the live London), position and rotation only; and, as `london-1.json` did, the
  spawn protection zones around 0.32a4's own spawn places (an `effect_zone` and its `inv_collision`,
  in day 2, 3, 5 and rain and evening 2, 3) go: the live London has none.
- `ffa-london-v032-1.json`: Free for All's own project `v032a_level_09/day_8`, a copy of day 4 as the
  rows above leave it (as `ffa-projects-1` copies every map's), with the live Free for All's 50 spawns
  (the owner's picks, `ffa-london-picks-1`, with the radii and the 500 ms of protection the FFA lists
  gave them), and without ammo crates (`make_no_crates_edits`' rule: both of 0.32a4's crates whole).
- The HUD: London's minimap and frame for `v032a_level_09` (0.32a4's own `map_09.png` is the same
  picture); the wiki's top-down render of `v032a_level_09` in that frame.

Not carried: patch 14's fixes of the old London's look (`london-tone-1`, `london-environment-1`,
`london-probe-*`, `london-probes-1`, `london-vines-1`, `london-vertex-blend-1`): 0.32a4's own
environment, probes and materials replace them, with London's tone marker and box-projected probes
carried by the manifest as for London (v0.42). The campfires are 0.32a4's own fires with their damage
zones (one mechanism), not `port-campfires-1`.
