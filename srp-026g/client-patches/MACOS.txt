SURVARIUM 0.26g - MACOS

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Needs Rosetta 2, installed once
in Terminal with:

    softwareupdate --install-rosetta --agree-to-license

START

Unpack the Skhid archive into an empty folder with about 10 GB free, then
double-click Skhid.command. The first time, macOS may refuse to open it
("could not verify"): open System Settings > Privacy & Security, choose
Open Anyway, and double-click it again. Or, in Terminal in that folder:

    bash Skhid.sh launcher

The launcher downloads the game and updates; press PLAY when ready.
For an existing game, apply the complete archive over its folder.
To play directly without checking for updates, run bash Skhid.sh, or run
bash play_skhid_mac.sh from game/binaries/x86.

The first launch downloads Wine (built by Sikarugir from CrossOver's
sources) and DXMT, which runs the game's Direct3D 11 on Metal: about
260 MB into ~/Library/Application Support/Skhid. It also creates a Wine
prefix in game/binaries/x86/wineprefix and installs the font Calibri
(a 27 MB download) that the game's text needs. Later launches reuse them.

DISPLAY AND KEYS

The first launch starts the game in fullscreen at the resolution of the
main display (the one with the menu bar). Change it in the game's Settings.
Settings: ~/Library/Application Support/Skhid/user.cfg.
In fullscreen the game takes the whole display: no menu bar or Dock (if
the menu bar still shows over it, click the game once); use Cmd+Tab to
switch away. On a MacBook with a notch, the top centre of the
picture is under the notch.
Sharper picture on a Retina display, at four times the pixels to draw:
start with SKHID_RETINA=1, for example SKHID_RETINA=1 bash Skhid.sh launcher,
then choose the larger resolution in the game's Settings.
Option works as Alt in the game.

IF IT FAILS

Report skhid-wine.log and, for update failures, updater_skhid.log from
game/binaries/x86. If the prefix is broken, close the game and launcher and
rename game/binaries/x86/wineprefix to an unused backup name; the next
launch makes a new one. Settings stored outside the prefix survive.

For a crash a developer asked to investigate, start with full-memory dumps:

    SKHID_CRASH_DUMP=full bash Skhid.sh launcher
