The 14 tiles of this folder (artists/catalog.tsv) are camouflage textures drawn by artists and
published under free licences, picked by the owner on 2026-11-26. classics_art.py fetch downloads
each source and checks its sha256; classics_art.py build then reproduces every WebP byte for byte
(checked 2026-11-26 with NumPy 2.4.4, Pillow 13.0.0 and SciPy 1.17.1; the Nobiax archive is read
with the 7z tool).

Each source is a seamless tile and is used whole, scaled to 768 px (the Commons drawings rendered
at 1920 px), save the two star patterns: a square of 30% of the drawing's side is cut and made
seamless as in classics/ (SOURCES.txt there). None of the sources discloses AI-generated or
AI-assisted work.

These textures are adaptations, and each is licensed as its source: blue-brush.webp under CC BY 3.0,
which asks only for the credit above; the six from CC BY-SA 4.0 drawings under CC BY-SA 4.0, or, as
CC BY-SA 4.0 permits for adaptations, under the GNU GPL v3 (this repository's licence). Each credit
applies wherever the texture goes, including the game's archive built from it. Hazard Pixel and Teal
Pixel are recolours of the Russian EMR digital pattern.


Looked at and left out: textures that disclose AI-generated or AI-assisted work (oleekconder,
SM ART'S, berserkerbones, Wayfold Studio and Megabyte Games on itch.io), packs without a licence,
paid packs that forbid redistributing the textures (this repository publishes the tiles),
ShareTextures (its "CC0" forbids redistribution without written permission), textures under
512 px, and the artist candidates the owner dropped on 2026-11-26.
