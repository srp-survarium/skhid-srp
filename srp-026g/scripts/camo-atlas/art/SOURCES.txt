87 tiles preserve the creator's original colorways. 33 use atlas color ramps.
Another 75 apply atlas inks and overprints to source motifs. The importer
selects the precise archive PNG, realization and print treatment for each row.

90 tiles use original artwork across ten textile, cartographic and mineral
families. They are drawn by expanded_art.py. Each catalog row links the
museum object, USGS description or mineral specimen used as a visual
reference. The paintings, photographs and maps remain on their source sites.

The first 120 WebPs are 1024 x 1024. Rows 121 to 285 are 768 x 768.
The game builds its own 512 x 512 diffuse textures from these sources.
