10 of the 11 tiles of this folder (initial/catalog.tsv) are procedural: initial_art.py draws
each one from seeded noise and the three to five colours of its catalog row. It reads no
image or other file, so no third-party artwork is in them. It reproduces those 10 WebPs byte
for byte (checked 2026-11-26 with NumPy 2.4.4 and Pillow 13.0.0). On 2026-11-26 the owner
dropped three more studies (Reed Rain 2805, Night Grid 2807, Violet Splinter 2811); their
ids stay unused.

The 11th, mm-14.webp (item 2812), is the real Ukrainian MM-14, cut from a photograph on
Wikimedia Commons by classics_art.py: File:MM14.jpg by Lucas Cavalcante Mesquita, CC BY-SA 4.0,
modified (cropped, made seamless, scaled). Its credit and licence are in classics/SOURCES.txt.
It replaced Pixel Steppe, this script's study of MM-14, under the same item on 2026-11-26.

The procedural tiles were made on 2026-11-25 as colourway proposals for the camouflage gallery that came
before this pack: a coding agent wrote the script and chose the names and palettes. The
script's only copy was in /tmp, and it was added here unchanged apart from its output path
and the removal of a preview contact sheet.

The source_url and source_label of each catalog row point to the camouflage family or
issued pattern the tile was meant to recall (TURBO DESIGN, Made Good Designs, British
Material Unit, the U.S. Army, Soviet Uniform, Militaria Plaza, Canada's Department of
National Defence, Ukraine's Ministry of Defence). Nothing was downloaded from those pages
into the tiles. Field Mosaic is a colour study in the spirit of Ukraine's MM-25; it is not a
copy of the pattern, whose sample the Ministry has not published.

No licence has been stated for the 10 procedural tiles. The project owner has to set one.
