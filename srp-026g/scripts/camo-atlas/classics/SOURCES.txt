The 68 tiles of this folder (classics/catalog.tsv), initial/mm-14.webp and initial/mm-25.webp are cut
by classics_art.py from free swatches and pictures of real camouflage patterns. classics_art.py fetch
downloads the sources and checks each one's sha256; classics_art.py build then reproduces every WebP
byte for byte (checked 2026-11-26 with NumPy 2.4.4, Pillow 13.0.0 and SciPy 1.17.1).

Each swatch tile is a modified copy of its source: cropped, its lighting evened out where
the source is a photograph (tropentarn, balkan-dpm), made seamless and scaled to 768 px. A
crop is made periodic by replacing a strip at its left and top edges with the pixels that
followed the crop in the swatch, cut along the path of least colour difference, so the tile's
edges meet as the swatch's own did. Centre Europe and the desert swatch are themselves
repeats, and their crops run over the swatch's edges. The vector drawings of K07 Dark, K20, K20
Navy and the three Ukrainian vehicle patterns are seamless tiles, rendered by Wikimedia Commons
at 1920 px and used whole (scaled). The 56 rows from id 3115 on were added on 2026-11-26 from the
owner's picks of the real-pattern candidates; their crops are the ones the owner was shown.

The ERDL and DCU files are flat copies of the two U.S. Army designs, which Commons files as
public domain (PD-USGov-Military-Army, PD-US-1978-89): the ERDL one was taken from a print
design on Spoonflower, the DCU one from a fabric shop's product image (both linked on their
Commons pages). A faithful copy of a two-dimensional public-domain design adds no copyright of
its own in the United States. The same holds for the ERDL lowland swatch. The blue tigerstripe is
a close-up from a U.S. Air Force photograph (af.mil, 2003) and keeps the "U.S. AIR FORCE" printed on
that fabric. The two lozenge drawings are of the German lozenge fabric of 1917-18; their Commons
pages give flight-depot.com as the source and no author.
