The 12 tiles of this folder (classics/catalog.tsv) and initial/mm-14.webp are cut by
classics_art.py from free swatches of real camouflage patterns. classics_art.py fetch downloads
the swatches and checks each one's sha256; classics_art.py build then reproduces every WebP
byte for byte (checked 2026-11-26 with NumPy 2.4.4, Pillow 13.0.0 and SciPy 1.17.1).

Each swatch tile is a modified copy of its source: cropped, its lighting evened out where
the source is a photograph (tropentarn, balkan-dpm), made seamless and scaled to 768 px. A
crop is made periodic by replacing a strip at its left and top edges with the pixels that
followed the crop in the swatch, cut along the path of least colour difference, so the tile's
edges meet as the swatch's own did. Centre Europe and the desert swatch are themselves
repeats, and their crops run over the swatch's edges.

The ERDL and DCU files are flat copies of the two U.S. Army designs, which Commons files as
public domain (PD-USGov-Military-Army, PD-US-1978-89): the ERDL one was taken from a print
design on Spoonflower, the DCU one from a fabric shop's product image (both linked on their
Commons pages). A faithful copy of a two-dimensional public-domain design adds no copyright of
its own in the United States.

These textures are adaptations, and each is licensed as its source: the five from CC BY-SA 4.0
sources under CC BY-SA 4.0, or, as CC BY-SA 4.0 permits for adaptations, under the GNU GPL v3
(this repository's licence); brushstroke.webp under CC BY-SA 3.0 only, since CC BY-SA 3.0 is not
compatible with the GPL. Each credit above applies wherever the texture goes, including the
game's archive built from it.

The CC0 drawings and the CC BY-SA swatches render national patterns (France's CCE and BME,
Hungary's 2015M, Ukraine's MM-14, Germany's Tropentarn, Bulgaria's DPM and splinter, the
Netherlands' NFP). The licences cover the drawings and photographs; whether an army claims a
design right in its pattern is not stated on those pages. The project owner accepted that.


Items 3107-3110 were procedural studies of classic patterns (Frogskin, Alpenflage, KLMK,
Leibermuster); the owner dropped them on 2026-11-26 and their ids stay unused. Item 3106 was
such a study (Brushstroke) until the real Rhodesian swatch above took its place.


Looked at and left out: SnowyCocoon's free packs (every motif is already in art/), ShareTextures
(its "CC0" adds use and redistribution limits), Megabyte Games' Chromatic Camouflage (CC BY 4.0,
disclosed as AI-assisted), photographs of folded garments or blurred ones, recolours of a
pattern chosen here (ERDL lowland, M81, CCE Daguet, Alpin and Montagne, BME variants, NFP
green and multitone), a second flecktarn (Japanese) beside the stock game's, Waffen-SS patterns,
and restricted designs (MARPAT, CADPAT, MultiCam and OCP, commercial hunting patterns).
MM-25: the Ministry of Defence of Ukraine has not published the pattern, and its fabric and
garment specifications set colours by an approved physical sample, with no picture of it.
