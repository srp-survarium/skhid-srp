The 12 tiles of this folder (classics/catalog.tsv) are drawn by classics_art.py: seven
are cut from free swatches of real camouflage patterns, five are original procedural studies of
classic patterns. classics_art.py fetch downloads the swatches and checks each one's sha256;
classics_art.py build then reproduces every WebP here byte for byte (checked 2026-11-26 with
NumPy 2.4.4, Pillow 13.0.0 and SciPy 1.17.1).

Swatches, all from Wikimedia Commons (the page is each catalog row's source_url):

  lizard.webp              French_Lizard_Pattern_Swatch_from_the_1950s.png, by VoltigeurFR,
                           CC0 1.0 (the 3840 px standard thumbnail of the 11202 px original)
  erdl-highland.webp       Brown-dominant "highland" ERDL camouflage pattern swatch.svg, uploaded by
                           Illegitimate Barrister: public domain, a U.S. Government design
                           (Engineer Research and Development Laboratories); 3840 px rendering
  three-color-desert.webp  DCU_camo_swatch.png, uploaded by Illegitimate Barrister: public domain, the U.S.
                           Army's three-colour desert pattern; the original file
  urban-brush.webp         Phase_I_All-Over_Brush_Urban_Camo_Close-Up_Swatch.png, uploaded by
                           SergeantSelfExplanatory: public domain, a U.S. Army photograph (from a
                           briefing on its 2002 Universal Camouflage for the Future Warrior trial)
                           of a trial pattern; 1280 px thumbnail
  centre-europe.webp       Bariolage_Centre-Europe.png, by Youri BRIAND, CC0 1.0; the original
  all-terrain.webp         Bariolage_multi-environnement_(B.M.E.).png, by Youri BRIAND, CC0 1.0;
                           the original
  danube-leaf.webp         2015M.png, by Shotya, CC0 1.0; the original

The ERDL and DCU files are flat copies of the two U.S. Army designs, which Commons files as
public domain (PD-USGov-Military-Army, PD-US-1978-89): the ERDL one was taken from a print
design on Spoonflower, the DCU one from a fabric shop's product image (both linked on their
Commons pages). A faithful copy of a two-dimensional public-domain design adds no copyright of
its own in the United States.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
A work of the U.S. Government is in the public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105).

The three CC0 drawings render current national patterns: the French Army's Centre Europe (CCE)
and Bariolage multi-environnement (BME), and the Hungarian Defence Forces' 2015M. The uploaders
dedicated their drawings to the public domain; whether the armies claim a design right in the
patterns themselves is not stated on those pages. The project owner decides whether to ship them.

Each crop is made periodic by replacing a strip at its left and top edges with the pixels that
followed the crop in the swatch, cut along the path of least colour difference, so the tile's
edges meet as the swatch's own did. Centre Europe and the desert swatch are themselves repeats,
and their crops run over the swatch's edges.

Studies (brushstroke, frogskin, alpenflage, klmk, leibermuster): drawn from the seeds and
palettes in classics_art.py; they read no image. Each recalls a classic family (the Rhodesian
brushstroke, the 1940s U.S. spot patterns, the Swiss TAZ 83, the Soviet KLMK, the 1945 German
Leibermuster) without copying a swatch of it. They were written on 2026-11-26 by a coding agent,
like initial/; no licence has been stated for them yet, as for initial/.

Looked at and left out: SnowyCocoon's free packs (every motif is already in art/), ShareTextures
(its "CC0" adds use and redistribution limits), Megabyte Games' Chromatic Camouflage (CC BY 4.0,
disclosed as AI-assisted), CC BY-SA swatches, photographs of folded garments, recolours of a
pattern chosen here (ERDL lowland, M81, CCE Daguet, Alpin and Montagne, BME variants), and
restricted designs (MARPAT, CADPAT, MultiCam and OCP, commercial hunting patterns).
