## Terms

- **Anomaly**: a zone that hurts while you stand in it. [Anomalies](anomalies)
- **Armour**: cuts a hit by how far it exceeds the penetration. [Damage](damage)
- **Armour penetration**: what a bullet takes off the armour first. [Damage](damage)
- **Artefact**: grows in anomalies; carried for its effect. [Artefacts](artefacts)
- **Battery**: carried to your container in Protective device. [Batteries](batteries)
- **Booster**: a pocket item with a fixed bonus. [Supplies](supplies)
- **Bundle**: items sold together for gold. [Shop](shop)
- **Charger**: gives batteries during a chargers match. [Batteries](batteries)
- **Condition**: how worn an item is; repairs bring it back. [Upgrades](upgrades)
- **Container**: where a team puts its batteries. [Batteries](batteries)
- **Defect**: a negative modification from wear or a drop. [Modifications](modifications)
- **Donor**: the second copy an upgrade uses up. [Upgrades](upgrades)
- **Drop**: an item a match gives. [Drops](drops)
- **Faction**: a seller with reputation ranks. [Factions](factions)
- **Gold**: premium money; 1 gold = {{exchange.rate}} silver. [Silver and gold](exchange)
- **Injury**: an empty leg or hand, or a hit head. [Healing](healing)
- **Isolation**: armour against anomalies: fire, electric, toxins, radiation. [Anomalies](anomalies)
- **Item level**: how strong an item is; rooms can cap it. [Rooms](rooms)
- **Kit**: one of your {{kits}} loadouts. [Kits](kits)
- **Late join**: coming into a match already played. [Rooms](rooms)
- **Modification**: a bonus on a weapon or gear. [Modifications](modifications)
- **Pain**: a pool every hit drains; empty, you die. [Damage](damage)
- **Premium**: more silver, experience, drops. [Shop](shop)
- **Rank**: your reputation level with a faction. [Factions](factions)
- **Room**: a group that plays match after match. [Rooms](rooms)
- **Silver**: the money of matches and the shop. [Silver and gold](exchange)
- **Spot**: marking an enemy or a battery. [Spotting](spotting)
- **Tickets**: a team's lives in Team Deathmatch. [Game modes](modes)
- **Toxins**: the pool toxic and radiation damage drain; it does not recover by itself. [Healing](healing)
- **Upgrade**: one more modification from a donor, or one less. [Upgrades](upgrades)
