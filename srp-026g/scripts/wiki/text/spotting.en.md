# Spotting
section: mechanics
summary: Marking enemies and batteries.

- One press of the spot key, one spot: the first enemy or battery your view meets within 100 m.
- No cooldown: every press counts, hit or miss.
- An enemy stays marked for 10 s; spotting them again starts the 10 s again.
- Teammates are never spotted; the spot goes past them.
- Aiming at any part of a battery spots it. Markers: [Batteries](batteries).
- Only a battery's first spotter is awarded.
