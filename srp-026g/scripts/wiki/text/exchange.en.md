# Silver and gold
section: economy
summary: The exchange and what gold is for.

- 1 gold = {{exchange.rate}} silver, both ways.
- Click the gold or silver box at the top, or **Get Gold** in any "not enough gold" dialog: the exchange opens.
- Typing more than you can pay for gives the most you can, at once. Silver always turns into whole gold.
- Gold buys premium items, bundles and premium time: [Shop](shop).
- A new account gets {{signup.silver}} and {{signup.gold}}: [New account](new-account).
- A skills reset costs {{skills.reset_silver}} or {{skills.reset_gold}}.
