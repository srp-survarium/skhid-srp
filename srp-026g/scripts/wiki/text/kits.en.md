# Kits
section: mechanics
summary: Your loadouts, and switching before the start.

- {{kits}} kits, all usable by everyone.
- A kit that can't play (no weapon, too little ammo) keeps you out of a room's match.
- Before the match starts you can switch kits as often as you like, while you have done nothing yet: no shot, no hit, no damage taken.
- A switch respawns you in the same place, same facing, with what is left of your spawn protection. No death, no timer.
- After the start: pick the next life's kit on the death screen; the last pick wins.
- Late joiners start with their first kit and can't switch before their first death.
- While the kit picker is open before the start, drag to turn the camera and scroll to zoom, as in the lobby.
