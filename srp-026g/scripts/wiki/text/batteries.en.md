# Batteries
section: mechanics
summary: Protective device: carry batteries to your container.

The [Protective device](modes) modes. Where everything is: [the Maps tab](/maps).

- Each team has a **container**. A battery in your container is a point for your team.
- {{mode.batteries}} batteries a match. A team with all of them in its container wins, 3 s later.
- Time up ({{mode.match_time}}): more batteries wins; equal is a draw.
- Taking a battery out of a container takes that point away.
- Picking one up takes {{battery.take_time}}; putting it into a container {{battery.put_time}}.
- Carrying one: movement speed {{battery.speed}}.
- Dropped or carried by a player who dies: it lies where it fell.
- After the last battery goes in, no container can be used.

## Spotting a battery

- Aim at the battery, its body too, and press spot: your team sees it. No cooldown.
- No marker at spawn; picking it up wipes the markers; after a drop the dropper's team sees it; a battery in a container has none.
- Only the battery's first spotter gets the spot award. See [Spotting](spotting).

## Protective device

The batteries lie at their spawn points from the start.

## Protective device: chargers

- Batteries come out of **chargers** during the match: {{mode.chargers_working}} chargers work at once.
- A working charger gives a battery every {{mode.charger_every}}, give or take {{mode.charger_variation}}.
- The goal is the same: all {{mode.batteries}} in your container.
