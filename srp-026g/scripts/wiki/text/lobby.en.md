# Lobby tips
section: mechanics
summary: Buying, selling and the windows that differ from the original.

- Right-click an owned item with modifications: **Buy** buys a stock copy; yours stays as it is. No "already purchased" question.
- In the upgrade window, right-click an item of its list: **Buy** or **Sell**.
- **Rooms** replaces Map in the top bar: [Rooms](rooms).
- **Get Gold** opens the exchange: [Silver and gold](exchange).
