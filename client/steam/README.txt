SKHID ON STEAM

Put these files in your Skhid game folder, beside Skhid.bat, and double-click
skhid-steam.bat. If Survarium is in your Steam library and installed, its Play
button then starts Skhid; if not, a game named "Survarium" is added to your
library. Either way Steam shows you playing Survarium while you play on Skhid.
Steam must be closed while it runs (it waits for that). To undo it, double-click
skhid-steam-remove.bat. Steam's files it changes are copied first to
*.before-skhid in Steam\userdata\<your id>\config.
